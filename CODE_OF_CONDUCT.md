# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the sysreqR project a respectful,
welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

* use welcoming and inclusive language;
* respect differing viewpoints and experiences;
* give and accept constructive feedback professionally;
* focus on what is best for the project and its users;
* show empathy toward people who are new to R, GNU/Linux, or open source.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

* harassment, intimidation, threats, or personal attacks;
* discriminatory language or behavior;
* sexualized language or imagery;
* publishing another person's private information without permission;
* sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or pull requests.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at
a.sofimahmudi@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with
care for the safety and privacy of the people involved.

Project maintainers may remove comments, close issues or pull requests, block
participants, or take other appropriate action when this code of conduct is
violated.

## Attribution

This code of conduct is adapted from common open source community standards and
the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1.
